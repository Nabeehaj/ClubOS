# SBCS data used in the demo

The source workbook is `ClubsOS spreadsheet.xlsx`, supplied by the team on
2026-10-24. We use only its SBCS row (Sheet1, row 5) and its SBCS event entry
(row 30); the workbook itself is not copied into this repository.

## Public facts shown in ClubOS

- Club name and contact email: Stony Brook Computing Society (SBCS),
  `sbcomputingsociety@cs.stonybrook.edu`. The email and club description also
  appear on [Stony Brook's SBCS page](https://linguistics.stonybrook.edu/commcms/ceas-undergrad/student_organization/sbcs.php).
- SBUHacks 2026, October 23–25, with the Melville Library Central Reading Room
  and SAC Auditorium. The dates and venue are on the
  [official event site](https://hack.sbcs.io/) and a
  [Stony Brook Libraries announcement](https://library.stonybrook.edu/2026/10/07/steel-and-steam-at-this-years-sbuhacks/).
- The workbook lists Eduardo Lozano as President with a Stony Brook email.
  His current role has not been independently verified; he is shown only as a
  workbook listing, not as a demo login, task assignee or author of messages.

## Not presented as confirmed SBCS records

- The workbook has conflicting meeting information: its club profile lists
  Monday 5–6 PM, while its event row lists Wednesday 5–6 PM and marks the
  recurrence unverified. The university page refers to Wednesdays in **Fall
  2025**, not a confirmed Fall 2026 schedule. ClubOS therefore shows no current
  meeting time.
- The workbook displays an $8,500 USG budget in H5 but its own note says budget
  allocations could not be read or verified. We do not treat $8,500 as a fact.
- The vendor, purchases and $4,500 event budget in the SBUHacks row are
  explicitly marked demo data by the workbook.
- ClubOS's seven sample logins, board roles, tasks, messages, approvals, and
  all financial figures are fictional UI scenarios. They are not SBCS records.
